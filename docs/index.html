Lair

Talk to all your coding agents by voice, side by side, from the menu bar. Speech-to-text and voices run on your Mac.

Get it on GitHub Build it

What it does

Hold ⌥ Space and talk

Ask without leaving what you're doing. The agent answers out loud; talk over it to cut it off.

Several agents at once

Each works on its own. Only the one in focus speaks; the others say in one line when they're done or need you.

Switch the natural way

Start a sentence with a name ("Iris, …"), click it in the panel, or click its column in Mission Control.

It asks before it acts

Approve by voice, in the panel, from the notification or in Mission Control. Nothing is approved for you.

Mission Control ⌥ ⌘ L

Every agent's whole stream side by side: tool calls, diffs, thinking, timings and cost.

A floating orb

During a call it floats above every window. Click it to talk, click again to send.

Model and thinking by voice

"Switch to Opus", "think harder", "go fast": or pick from the model line.

On your Mac

Parakeet hears you and Kokoro speaks, on the Neural Engine. Links and paths show as chips, never read aloud.

Build it

macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon, with Swift 6.2 (Xcode 26).

git clone https://github.com/areguig/lair.git
cd lair
scripts/build-app.sh       # builds and installs ~/Applications/Lair.app
open ~/Applications/Lair.app

The first launch downloads the speech models. Add agents in Settings…, or edit ~/.config/lair/config.json; secrets go in the Keychain.

Built on FluidAudio, Parakeet, Kokoro and KeyboardShortcuts.
